This page writes to the board over Web Serial, which only desktop Chrome and Edge have. Open it in one of them.

1 Choose your build

The Hardware Profile tells the firmware how your matrix is wired. It is stored on the board, apart from the firmware, so later updates keep it unless you choose another one here.

Hardware Profile

The current cap is what the matrix may draw. On a 500 mA USB port, leave about 100 mA for the board and 1 mA for every LED that stays dark, e.g. a built-in matrix you don't use.
